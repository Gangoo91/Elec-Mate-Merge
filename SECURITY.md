# Security Policy

Elec-Mate takes the security of our platform and our users' data seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability, please report it to us
privately. **Do not open a public GitHub issue, pull request, or discussion for
security reports.**

Email: **info@elec-mate.com**

Please include:

- A description of the vulnerability and its potential impact
- Steps to reproduce (proof-of-concept where possible)
- Any relevant logs, URLs, or screenshots
- Your name/handle if you would like to be credited

We aim to acknowledge reports within **3 working days** and to keep you updated
as we investigate and remediate.

## Scope

In scope:

- The Elec-Mate web application (elec-mate.com)
- The Elec-Mate iOS and Android applications
- Backend APIs and Supabase Edge Functions serving the applications

Out of scope:

- Social engineering, physical attacks, and denial-of-service testing
- Automated scanner output without a demonstrated, exploitable impact
- Reports affecting only unsupported or end-of-life browsers

## Responsible disclosure

Please give us a reasonable opportunity to remediate an issue before any public
disclosure. We will not pursue action against researchers who report in good
faith, act in accordance with this policy, and avoid privacy violations,
data destruction, or service disruption.

Thank you for helping keep Elec-Mate and our users safe.
