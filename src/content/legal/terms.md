**How to cancel.**

When you cancel, your subscription stops renewing and you keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.

**Your 14-day right to change your mind.** If you're a consumer, you can cancel within **14 days of signing up**. Cancelling during the free trial costs nothing. If your first payment has already been taken by then, email **info@elec-mate.com** within those 14 days and we'll refund it.

**After that.** Payments aren't refundable, and we don't refund part-used months or years — when you cancel, you keep access until the end of the period you've paid for. This doesn't affect your legal rights if the service isn't as described or we can't provide it.

**Business customers.** The 14-day right is a consumer right. If you subscribe for your business, payments aren't refundable except as our [business terms](/business-terms) say.
