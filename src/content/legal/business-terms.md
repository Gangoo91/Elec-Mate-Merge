## The short version

- These terms apply when an **organisation** — an electrical business, a college or a training provider — sets up Elec-Mate for its people. Our [terms of service](/terms) still apply; these add to them.
- **You** decide who joins and what you use the information for. **We** run the service and look after the data for you under our [Data Processing Agreement](/dpa).
- What you pay, for how many people and for how long is set out in your **order** — an email from us, a quote, an invoice or a discount code. If anything in the order differs from these terms, the order wins.
- These terms take priority over any purchase order or terms of your own, unless we've agreed otherwise in writing.
- Either of us can end the arrangement with **30 days' notice**. You can take your data with you.

## 1. Who these terms are between

**Elec-Mate Ltd** (company number 16416291, 33 Gable Road, Whitehaven, CA28 8HE) and the organisation named in the order ("**you**"). The person accepting confirms they're authorised to bind the organisation.

## 2. What we provide

- **Employers:** accounts or discount codes for your electricians and apprentices, and access to the Employer hub — your team, jobs, timesheets, location check-ins and site safety tools.
- **Colleges and training providers:** tutor accounts, discount codes for learners, and the College hub — learners' progress, attendance, results, off-the-job hours and portfolios.

The Employer hub is in **early access**: we're building it with the firms using it, features may change, and we'll tell you before we remove anything you rely on.

- **Late payment.** We may charge statutory interest and compensation under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998 on overdue invoices, and suspend access if an invoice is more than 14 days overdue after we've reminded you.
- **Renewal.** Orders for a fixed period renew automatically for the same period unless either of us gives notice before the renewal date.
- **Fees are non-refundable** except as section 7 says.
- **Free accounts** we give to staff (for example tutor accounts) are provided on the understanding that they're used with your learners. We may review them if they aren't used.

## 4. Your people

- You choose who to add and remove. When someone leaves, remove them — their personal Elec-Mate account (if they have one) stays theirs.

- You're responsible for how your people use Elec-Mate on your behalf, and for keeping logins secure.

## 5. Data protection

- For the information you see about your people in the Employer or College hub (timesheets, location check-ins, progress, results, portfolios), **you're the controller** and we're your processor under our [Data Processing Agreement](/dpa).
- You must have a lawful basis for that processing and tell your people what you use it for — for example in your staff or learner privacy notice. **Location check-ins** must only be used for legitimate purposes such as safety and timekeeping, and never outside working hours.
- Learners may be **16 or 17**. You'll take the extra care the law expects with young people's data, and so will we.
- Wellbeing information (mood, journal, peer support) is **never** shown to employers or colleges.

## 6. Service and support

- We work to keep Elec-Mate available and fix problems quickly, but we don't promise uninterrupted service — for example during maintenance or problems with our providers.
- Support is by email at **info@elec-mate.com**. We aim to reply within **one working day**, and faster for anything stopping your team working. These are targets, not guarantees.
- We'll tell you about any **planned downtime** we expect to affect working hours in advance.
