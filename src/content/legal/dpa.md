## About this agreement

When you store details about your clients, their properties, your workers or other people in Elec-Mate, **you're the controller** of that personal data and **Elec-Mate Ltd is your processor**. UK law (Article 28 of the UK GDPR) requires a written contract between us. This is it.

It forms part of our [terms of service](/terms) and applies automatically to every account that stores other people's personal data — you accept it when you accept the terms. If your organisation needs a signed copy, email **info@elec-mate.com**.

**Processor:** Elec-Mate Ltd, company number 16416291, 33 Gable Road, Whitehaven, CA28 8HE. ICO registration ZB935897.
**Controller:** the account holder (you, or the organisation you act for).

## 1. What we process

| | |
|---|---|
| **Subject matter** | Providing Elec-Mate to you |
| **Duration** | While your account is open, plus up to 30 days after deletion (section 9) |
| **Nature and purpose** | Storing, organising, displaying, transmitting, rendering into documents and, when you use AI features, analysing the data, so you can create certificates, reports, quotes, invoices, schedules, risk assessments and records, and manage your work and team |

## 2. Your instructions

We process this data **only on your documented instructions** — which are these terms, this agreement, and the way you use Elec-Mate — unless UK law requires otherwise, in which case we'll tell you first if the law allows. If we think an instruction breaks data protection law, we'll tell you.

You're responsible for having a lawful basis to hold the data, for what you enter, and for giving the people concerned the information the law requires.

## 3. Confidentiality

Everyone at Elec-Mate who can access the data is bound to keep it confidential, and access is limited to what's needed to run and support the service.

## 4. Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures (Article 32), including:

- encryption of data in transit and at rest;
- database rules that restrict each account's data to that account and the people it authorises;
- passwords stored only as secure hashes, and optional biometric sign-in on devices;
- limited staff access, kept to what's needed;
- error monitoring that masks on-screen text and images;
- keeping our software and dependencies patched;
- backups kept for a limited period and overwritten in rotation.

## 5. Sub-processors

You give us **general authorisation** to use the sub-processors listed on our **[sub-processors page](/subprocessors)**. Before we add or replace a sub-processor that handles your data, we'll update that page and notify you by email or in the app, giving you at least **14 days** to object. If you object on reasonable data-protection grounds and we can't resolve it, your remedy is to stop using the affected feature or cancel.

We put a written contract with each sub-processor with data protection obligations equivalent to these, and we remain responsible for them.

## 6. International transfers

Your data is stored in the UK (London). Where a sub-processor processes it outside the UK, we make sure a valid transfer mechanism is in place: UK adequacy regulations (for example the EU), the UK–US data bridge, or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or UK Addendum.

## 7. Helping you

Taking into account what we can reasonably do as your processor, we'll help you:

- respond to requests from people exercising their rights (access, correction, deletion and so on) — most of which you can handle yourself in the app;
- meet your security, breach-notification and data-protection-impact-assessment obligations.

If someone contacts us directly about data you control, we'll pass the request to you and won't answer it ourselves unless you ask us to.

## 8. Personal data breaches

If we become aware of a personal data breach affecting your data, we'll tell you **without undue delay** (and in any case in time for you to meet your own 72-hour duty to the ICO), with what we know about what happened, the data and people affected, the likely consequences and what we're doing about it — and we'll keep you updated.

## 9. When our contract ends

When you delete your account, your data is removed from the app straight away and permanently erased within **30 days**; backups are overwritten in rotation after that. Before you delete it, you can export your certificates, quotes and invoices as PDFs and download your account data. We don't keep copies unless UK law requires us to.

## 10. Audits

We'll make available the information you reasonably need to show that we meet these obligations, and we'll answer reasonable written questions about our security. If that isn't enough, we'll allow an audit by you or an independent auditor you appoint, with reasonable notice, at your cost, during business hours, no more than once a year, and under confidentiality.

## 11. Liability and law

Each of us is responsible for our own compliance with data protection law. Liability under this agreement is subject to the limits in our [terms of service](/terms), except where the law doesn't allow those limits. This agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales.
