These are the companies that process personal data on our behalf. Each is bound by a contract that requires it to protect the data and use it only to provide its service to us. Transfers outside the UK are covered as set out in our [privacy notice](/privacy#8-international-transfers).

If we add or replace a sub-processor that handles your clients' data, we'll update this page and, for business customers under our [Data Processing Agreement](/dpa), give notice so you can object.

## Hosting and core service

| Service | What it does | Data it receives | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| Supabase | Database, sign-in, file storage, server functions | All account and app data | Stored in the UK (London); Supabase is a US and Singapore company |
| Vercel | Website hosting and delivery | Requests to our website (IP address, browser) | Global network, USA company |

## Payments

| Service | What it does | Data it receives | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stripe | Subscription payments, and card payments you take from your clients | Name, email, billing details, payment records | UK and Ireland, with transfers to the USA |
| Apple and Google | In-app purchases on iPhone and Android | Purchase records (handled under Apple's and Google's own terms) | USA |
| RevenueCat | Managing in-app subscriptions | App user ID, purchase records | USA |

## Email, messages and notifications

| Service | What it does | Data it receives | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brevo | All our emails — receipts, reminders, documents you send to clients, and marketing | Email address, name, email content, preferences | EU (France) |
| Twilio or Vonage | Text-message verification codes (one or the other) | Phone number | USA / UK |
| Twilio | Texts with your clients, missed calls and voicemail on your Elec-Mate number, and WhatsApp messages when they are sent through Twilio | Your clients' phone numbers, the content of texts and WhatsApp messages, call details and voicemail recordings | USA (Twilio Inc.), unless Twilio's Ireland region is used for texts |
| Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform) | Sending and receiving WhatsApp messages with your clients | Your clients' WhatsApp numbers and profile names, message content and attachments (Meta keeps message content for up to 30 days) | USA, unless Meta's local storage in another country is switched on |
| Apple, Google and your browser's push service | App and browser notifications | Device token, notification content | USA |

## Services you choose to connect

When you connect **Google Calendar, Microsoft Outlook or Gmail, Xero, QuickBooks, Sage or FreshBooks**, data moves between Elec-Mate and that service on your instruction. Those companies are not our sub-processors — your use of them is under their own terms with you.
