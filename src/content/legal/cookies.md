## The short version

- Elec-Mate stores a few things on your device so it works — keeping you signed in, saving drafts so you don't lose work without signal, and remembering your settings. These are **essential** and don't need your consent.
- **Analytics** and **marketing** tracking are **optional**. On the website they're off until you say yes in the cookie banner.
- You can change your mind any time: **Settings → Privacy → Cookie preferences** in the app, or the cookie link at the bottom of our website.

"Cookies" here also covers similar technologies: browser storage (localStorage and IndexedDB), tracking pixels, and app software kits.

## Essential — always on

These make Elec-Mate work and keep it secure. The law allows them without consent because the service can't work without them.

| What | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|
| Sign-in session (stored in your browser or app, not a cookie) | Keeps you signed in securely | Until you sign out |
| Your cookie choices | Remembers what you chose in the banner | Until you change them or clear your browser |
| Offline drafts and saved work | Keeps certificates and forms safe when you lose signal, until they sync | Until synced or you sign out |
| App settings (theme, layout, last-used options) | Remembers how you like things | Until you clear your browser |
| Referral code from a friend's link | Makes sure your friend gets their referral credit when you join | Until you sign up |
| Sidebar cookie (`sidebar:state`) | Remembers whether the side menu is open | 7 days |
| Security and fraud checks by Stripe at checkout | Protects payments | Set by Stripe on its own checkout page |

## Error monitoring — always on, no personal data

We use **Sentry** to find and fix crashes. When something goes wrong, it records technical details and a short replay of the screen. **All text, typed input and images in replays are masked**, so it captures layout and clicks, not your data or your clients'.

## Visit statistics — always on, no cookies

**Vercel Analytics** counts page visits and page speed without cookies. Visitors are counted using a hash that's discarded every 24 hours, so you aren't tracked from day to day.

## Analytics — optional

| What | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|
| **PostHog** (stored in your browser) | Shows us which features are used and where people get stuck, including short screen recordings with all text, typing and images hidden | Up to 1 year |

Off until you accept analytics. If you say no, nothing is stored and no events are sent.

## Marketing — optional

| What | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|
| **Meta Pixel** (`_fbp`, `_fbc`) | Tells us which Facebook and Instagram adverts lead to sign-ups | Up to 90 days |
| **Google Ads / Google tag** (`_gcl_au`, `_ga`) | Tells us which Google adverts lead to sign-ups | Up to 2 years |
| **Campaign details** (where you came from: campaign name, ad click ID) | Lets us see which adverts and links work | Until you sign up, then kept with your account |
| **Sign-up and purchase events sent from our servers** to Meta and Google, with your email in a scrambled (hashed) form | Matches sign-ups and purchases to adverts | Kept by Meta and Google under their policies |

Off until you accept marketing. If you say no, none of these run — including the events sent from our servers.

## In the iPhone and Android apps

The apps use the same sign-in, offline storage and settings as above, plus **push notifications** if you allow them (you can turn these off in your phone's settings). On iPhone, we only use advertising measurement if you allow tracking when Apple asks.

## Videos

Training videos are played from **YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode**. YouTube doesn't set cookies until you play a video; when you do, YouTube's own privacy policy applies.

## Changing your choices

- **App:** Settings → Privacy → Cookie preferences.
- **Website:** the "Cookie preferences" link in the footer.
- **Your browser:** you can also block or delete cookies and site data in your browser settings — if you block essential storage, you may not be able to sign in.

Turning an option off stops it from then on; it doesn't undo what was collected before.
