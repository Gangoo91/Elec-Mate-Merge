## Delete your account in the app

1. Open Elec-Mate and sign in.
2. Go to **Settings → Privacy**.
3. Tap **Delete My Account** and confirm.

Can't sign in? Email **info@elec-mate.com** from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We'll confirm it's you, then delete it.

## Before you delete: export what you need

**Your certificates, quotes, invoices and client records are deleted with your account.** If you need to keep them — for example, certificates you've issued — download them first:

- **Certificates, quotes and invoices:** open each one and save or share the PDF.
- **Your account data:** **Settings → Privacy → Download My Data** gives you a ZIP file of your records (CSV spreadsheets and JSON) with links to your photos and files.

## Your subscription

- **Paid on the website (Stripe):** deleting your account cancels your subscription or free trial straight away. You won't be charged again.
- **Paid in the iPhone app:** cancel in your **Apple ID → Subscriptions**. Deleting your account or the app does **not** cancel an Apple subscription.
- **Paid in the Android app:** cancel in **Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions**. Deleting your account or the app does **not** cancel a Google Play subscription.

## What's deleted, and when

| | When |
|---|---|
| You're signed out and can't sign in again | Straight away |
| Your certificates, quotes, invoices, jobs, client records, photos and files | Kept for **30 days** in case you change your mind, then permanently erased |
| Learning progress, portfolio, CPD and wellbeing entries | Kept for **30 days**, then permanently erased |
| Database backups | Overwritten in rotation after that |

The 30 days gives you a short window to change your mind: email **info@elec-mate.com** within that time and we can restore your account.

## What we keep

- **Records of payments you made to us**, for **6 years**, because UK tax law requires it.
- A **record that you unsubscribed** from marketing, so we never email you again.
- **Anonymous statistics** that can't identify you.

## If someone added you to their team

If your employer or college gave you an account, deleting it removes your access. Records they keep about your work in their own systems are their responsibility — ask them.

More detail is in our [privacy notice](/privacy). Questions: **info@elec-mate.com**.
