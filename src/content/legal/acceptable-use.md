Elec-Mate is for apprentices, electricians, employers and colleges to learn, work and run their businesses. This policy is part of our [terms of service](/terms). Most of it is common sense.

## Don't

**Falsify anything**
- Issue a certificate for work you didn't inspect or test, or enter test results you didn't measure.
- Misrepresent your qualifications, your Elec-ID or who you are.
- Use someone else's name, signature or registration on a document.

**Misuse other people's data**
- Enter people's personal details without a lawful reason, or use client data for anything other than your work for them.
- Share other users' or clients' details without permission.
- Upload photos of people where they'd reasonably expect privacy, unless it's needed for the job.

**Break the law or harm people**
- Use Elec-Mate for anything illegal, fraudulent or to launder money.
- Harass, threaten or abuse anyone — including in peer support or team messages.
- Post content that's defamatory, obscene or discriminatory.

**Misuse AI features**
- Use Elec-AI or other AI tools to produce fake documents, mislead clients, or create harmful content.
- Present AI output as checked professional advice without checking it yourself.

**Abuse the platform**
- Share your login, or create accounts to get around limits, trials or offers.
- Scrape, copy or resell our courses, questions, guides or software, or use them to train AI.
- Try to break, probe or overload Elec-Mate, get around security, or reverse-engineer the apps.
- Upload malware, or use Elec-Mate to send spam.

## Do

- Keep your login secure and your details accurate.
- Check calculator and AI results before relying on them — you're responsible for your work.
- Follow safe isolation, manufacturers' instructions and the regulations, whatever the app says.
- Treat other people in Elec-Mate with respect.

## If the rules are broken

Depending on how serious it is, we may give a warning, remove content, suspend features, or close the account (see [terms](/terms#15-suspending-or-closing-accounts)). We'll report illegal activity — such as falsified safety certificates or fraud — to the relevant authorities.

## Reporting a problem

Email **info@elec-mate.com** with what happened, when, and any screenshots or links. If you've found a security weakness, please tell us privately at the same address before sharing it anywhere.

## If you're struggling

If you or someone you're talking to is in danger, call **999**. You can talk to **Samaritans** any time on **116 123** (free), or text **SHOUT to 85258**.
